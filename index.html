1. In 30 seconds

ProblemAgencies run their own outreach and every client's from one Saleshandy account. Nothing walls one client off from another, a client login can't be limited to its own work, and one client can use up the whole plan without the agency seeing who used it
OutcomeOne account holds many workspaces, one per client. Each is a full Saleshandy with its own data and people; nothing crosses between workspaces. The agency decides how much of its one plan each workspace may use. Client users see only their own workspace
What stays the sameNothing changes for an account until someone adds a workspace. New accounts no longer see Client Management
Who it's for202 paying accounts (7.0%) created a Client Management client on purpose (measured, Metabase, 16 Oct 2026). Agencies on the 2022 Agency Portal with 5 or more clients hold 15 client accounts at the median, 42 at p90 and 219 at most (measured, Metabase, 22 Oct 2026), so every list of workspaces needs search and paging

3. Flows

3.1 Adding the first workspace

Account Owners and Account Admins add workspaces. Before the first exists, + Add a workspace in the switcher (bottom left) is the only entry point.

  1. Confirm intent. Pop-up "Add a workspace?": one sentence that the user's work stays where it is, a picture of the account becoming "<account> (Your workspace)" beside a new workspace, and "You can delete it later." (D8). No numbers. Not now, Esc and close change nothing.
  2. Form. Name and website (§3.2).
  3. Add a workspace creates the workspace and converts the account in one all-or-nothing step. The new workspace starts with every limit at 0.
  4. Transition. Home's skeleton shows while the switcher opens once and the two levels step in; when the conversion finishes, the page lands on Home. No full-screen animation, copy or numbers; reduced motion fades.
  5. Failure. "We couldn't set up your workspaces. Your account is exactly as it was." with Retry; the form keeps its values. No screen ever shows a half-converted account.
  6. Guide. Account Owners and Account Admins get a five-step guide on Home that advances as they act: open the new workspace, invite its people (or Later), set its limits, open the workspace, switch back to Account. Skip ends it; the account menu replays it.

3.6 Setting and changing limits

Same rules in two places: Usage & limits (account level, one resource at a time, a row per workspace, your workspace's row edited like any other) and a workspace's Set limits / Limits tab.

  1. Click the pencil beside a limit and type a whole number.
  2. A save bar shows the change count. Apply writes every change at once; the toast's Undo restores every value.
  3. If a change stops a workspace that is sending (its new limit is at or below what it has used), Apply asks first: "Lower this limit?", listing "<workspace> stops sending until <renewal date>" ("until you raise it" for resources that don't renew). Buttons: Keep editing, Lower limit. The save bar says which workspaces' people get a notice.
  4. A stopped workspace is held, never paused (D10). Its sequences keep their status and resume on their own at the next scheduled send once there is room (Undo, a raise or the renewal). Nothing that fell due during the stop is sent twice.
