1. In 30 seconds

ProblemAgencies run their own outreach and every client's from one Saleshandy account. Nothing walls one client off from another, a client login can't be limited to its own work, and one client can use up the whole plan without the agency seeing who used it
OutcomeOne account holds many workspaces, one per client. Each is a full Saleshandy with its own data and users; nothing crosses between workspaces. The agency decides how much of its one plan each workspace may use. Client users see only their own workspace
What stays the sameUntil someone adds a workspace, the account works as today plus the workspace switcher. Client Management is hidden for new accounts and for accounts with no real Client Management client (§10.1)
Who it's for202 paying accounts, 7.0% of all paying accounts, created a Client Management client on purpose (measured, Metabase, 16 Oct 2026). Agencies on the 2022 Agency Portal with 5 or more clients hold 15 client accounts at the median, 42 at p90 and 219 at most (measured, Metabase, 22 Oct 2026), so every list of workspaces needs search and paging

2.2 The plan rule

Plan = every workspace's limit + Left to allocate, per resource. Your workspace has a limit like any other. Nothing left to allocate is used until it is given to a workspace (D1).

EventEffect
First workspace added (the account converts)Your workspace's limit is the whole plan; Left to allocate is 0. Nothing stops sending
New or restored workspaceEvery limit 0 (§3.3)
Give a workspace 10,000 emails (illustrative)Left to allocate falls by 10,000. Refused if not enough is left to allocate
Lower any limit, your workspace's includedThe unused part is left to allocate again: never more than the limit minus what that workspace has used this period
Set a limit at or below what that workspace has usedAllowed after the Lower limit confirm (§3.6); that resource stops there until the limit rises or the resource renews
Upgrade, add-on, credits bought from BillingLand in Left to allocate
Credits bought from inside a workspaceRaise that workspace's limit by the amount bought; the purchase confirm says so
Workspace disabledKeeps its limits, still counted against the plan, across renewals, until someone lowers its limit by typing
Delete a workspaceThe unused part of its limits is left to allocate again; what it used this period stays spent
Renewal of a resource that resetsOn that resource's own day (§5.1), every limit stays the same and every workspace's used goes to 0. What is still left to allocate on that resource is lost; Usage & limits shows it as Left to allocate, under a title line that says when the resource renews and that unused amounts don't carry over
Plan change, downgrade, plan end§5.2 and §5.3

Read-back (LI-1): per resource, the sum over every workspace, deleted ones included until the resource renews, of the larger of limit and used, plus Left to allocate, equals the plan, except in the over state, where it is larger and Left to allocate is 0. Left to allocate is never below 0.

2.4 Before and after the first added workspace

Before: Saleshandy as today, plus the workspace switcher (the tile at the foot of the left rail) with one row and "+ Add a workspace". After: the Admin Panel (Workspaces, Users, Usage & limits, Billing, Whitelabel, and 7 rows marked Soon) sits above the workspaces; all existing data is your workspace, unchanged; Users in the Admin Panel lists every user, and each workspace keeps Settings › Users & Teams, scoped to it (§3.9); Billing & Subscription leaves the workspace (§6 #1). The account stays converted even if every client workspace is later deleted (D8).
